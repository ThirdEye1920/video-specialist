# Video Specialist — by Vinay Bisht

**A video editor that lives in your chat.** Paste a transcript or shot notes, name the platform, and get a ready-to-execute **Edit Sheet** — hook options, cut list, caption script, on-screen text, reframe notes and a brand-consistency check — that you finish in *any* editor in minutes.

Works in **Claude Code**, the **Claude app** and **ChatGPT**. No new editing subscription. It learns how you like to work — on your machine.

## Who it's for

- **Founders & solo operators** editing their own content
- **Lean in-house teams** shipping daily short-form with one brand voice
- **Creators** — food, lifestyle, fashion, skincare, fitness, vlogging

## What it does

- **Turns footage into a plan** — transcript, shot notes or a description in; one Edit Sheet out
- **Keeps your voice** — a 60-second Brand Card is loaded once and every hook, caption and CTA is written from it
- **Works in any editor** — CapCut, Premiere, Descript, DaVinci, your phone. No editor? It picks a free one for your device and walks you through it
- **Learns how you work** — your card, preferences and what you change are remembered locally, never sent anywhere
- **Runs in your chat** — wherever you already work

## Install in 60 seconds

**Claude Code**
```bash
unzip Video-Specialist-by-Vinay-Bisht-v1.0.0.zip
cd Video-Specialist-by-Vinay-Bisht-v1.0.0
chmod +x install.sh && ./install.sh --user
```
Then say *"edit this for Reels"* and paste your transcript. (`--codex`, `--agents`, `--project`, `--path DIR` also supported.)

**Claude app (web / desktop)** — create a Project → paste the body of `SKILL.md` into Project instructions → add `templates/` and `references/` as Project knowledge.

**ChatGPT (web / desktop)** — create a Project or Custom GPT → paste the body of `SKILL.md` into Instructions → upload `templates/` and `references/` as Files.

## What it doesn't do

It plans the edit and remembers your preferences. It stops before creative strategy, the brief itself, knowing what *performed*, scored review, and automated execution — the parts of an end-to-end creative system built around a specific brand.
